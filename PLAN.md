# Plans

## A, geometry

- Shapes will be used to depict hair, eyes, eyebrows, a nose, and skin. They will be arranged in a way like a normal human, but using rectangles it will give the face a blocky feel.
- Branch A
- Make the face feel blocky.

## B, assets

- Images will be used to give the face a hat and ears. They will sit on top of the head and on the side of the head, respectively.
- Branch B
- Give a juxtaposition of realism between the face, hat, and ears.

## C, colors and curves

- A gradient from white to red on the eyes. A bezier curve for the eyes to make it droop.
- Branch C
- Make the face look very tired.
